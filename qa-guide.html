  7. 07

  8. 08

Not bugs — known and deliberate

Please don't spend time reporting these.

  • Campaigns have no city targeting. The brief asks for it, but nothing in the system knows where a player is, so the control would look like it worked and do nothing.
  • No forecast before launching a campaign. With no history, any number would be invented.
  • The dashboard isn't live. It's correct when you open or refresh it. Nothing pushes updates.
  • Multi-accounting isn't detectable. No IP, device fingerprint or login history is stored anywhere — that needs a privacy and data-retention decision first.
  • Pool changes aren't attributed beyond the activity log. You can see who added units; there's no per-pool ledger.
  • Suspended players keep their points and vouchers. Suspension stops earning and spending, not reading. Taking back what someone already paid for would be confiscation.
  • The look is deliberately plain. Game screens are faithful to the prototype; the surrounding pages use the colours but not the full hand-drawn treatment yet.
  • Everything is desktop-width-friendly but built phone-first. Try it at phone width — that's the target.

Reporting back

Most useful: which role, which step number, what you saw instead. A screenshot of the screen plus the terminal output at that moment usually pins it immediately. If something looks wrong but might be intended, say so — the boundary between the two is exactly where I want your read.
